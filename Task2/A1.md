## Capacity Planning

Calculate Expected Attempts per call 
1. Attempt 1 always occur
2. Attempt 2 failure chance = 75%
3. Attempt 3 failure chance = 75%^2

thus,
Expected[Attempts] = 1 + 0.75 + 0.75^2 = 2.3125

total expected attempts; 
200,000 * 2.3125 = 462,500

## Calculate Expected line occupancy : 
E[T] = 0.25(120) + 0.75(30) = 52.5 seconds

## Calculate total workload and concurrent lines

462,500 * 52.5 = 24,281,250

Total time = 10  * 3600 = 36,000 seconds

Required avg concurrent lines 

L = 24,281,250/3600 = 674.48 ~ 675 concurrent lines required
